About

I spent five years building AI inside Bangladesh's two largest telecom operators. I started with the models: deep-learning recommenders that choose offers for about 85 million subscribers every month. Then the work kept pulling me outward, into AI assistants and agentic systems for HR, finance and field engineers, into the GPU platform they run on, and into AI&I, the company-wide programme to get every employee using AI, where I was on the core team and taught 400 colleagues in hands-on workshops. I left Grameenphone as AI Product Manager.

In 2026 I moved to Dublin for an MSc in AI and NLP at DCU. After five years I knew what worked in production, and I didn't always know why. I'm here to close that gap, and to go deeper into engineering, not further from it.

I work best close to the people who will use the thing: prototype fast, demo early, test against a control group, and stay until it runs.

MedTrace: catching a copied medicine code on its second scan

Anti-counterfeit medicine tracing. Every pack gets a unique serial and an append-only record on a public blockchain: minted, handed off, dispensed.

Built
A Solana program with one account per pack serial, and a Next.js app with screens for the manufacturer, distributor, pharmacy and patient
How
A pack can be dispensed only once, so a clone is caught when it is scanned. Patients verify with a phone camera and need no wallet

Research, teaching and recognition

Research

UAV-Assisted Cooperative Caching on Network Edge Using Multi-Agent Actor-Critic Reinforcement Learning. IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, 72(2), 2022. First author. Q1 journal, 60+ citations.

Drones acting as mobile caches have to decide what to store and where to fly, and none of them can see the whole network. I framed this as a multi-agent decision problem, designed and implemented an actor-critic reinforcement learning method for it, built the simulation environment, and compared it with baseline caching policies.
